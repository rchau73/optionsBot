# Preliminary answers to the legal brief (L1–L24)

> **Not legal advice.** The legal-counsel skill's preparatory answers to every question in `docs/product/legal-brief.md` §3, so a licensed lawyer can confirm, correct or reject each one instead of starting from zero. Each answer gives the likely position, the reasoning and rules (details in `jurisdictions/`), our confidence (**high / medium / low**) and the structure we recommend until counsel answers. When counsel answers, the line becomes **Counsel (date): …** and the opinion goes in the gitignored `data/product/legal/`.

**Overall position until counsel answers:** internal use only (our own accounts and capital, testnet), no third-party money, no hosted service for anyone, no performance figures in marketing, no prices for an investment service. The first external step we recommend asking counsel to clear is a **testnet trial for named testers under a written agreement** (L6), then a **software-licence offer in Brazil** (L1 fallback), then a **partnership with an authorised gestora** for the full hosted product.

| **L1** Brazil: portfolio management or advice? | **Hosted service = *administração de carteiras*** (Res. CVM 21/2021): we would decide trades in derivatives (*valores mobiliários* under Lei 6.385/76 art. 2º, VIII) on the client's behalf; automation and the absence of custody don't change it; acting unregistered is a crime (art. 27-E). A customer-operated **licence** is likely outside, if we never operate the account, give no personal recommendation and promise no results. | medium-high / medium | Licence model only; no hosted service for Brazilians. |
| **L2** Brazil: VASP? | **Probably not**: we never exchange, transfer or custody assets, and the derivatives are CVM matter (Lei 14.478/2022 excludes securities from *ativo virtual*). Read the BCB's 2025 VASP rules for "administração" and foreign providers. | low (verify) | Same as L1. |
| **L3** EU: MiCA? | **MiCA does not apply** to the options: crypto derivatives are MiFID financial instruments, which MiCA art. 2(4)(a) excludes. MiCA matters only if we touch spot crypto. | high | No EU customers. |
| **L4** EU: MiFID? | **Yes**, the hosted service is portfolio management (Annex I A(4)); the customer's own key doesn't change it. Reverse solicitation is not a business model. A licence without personal recommendations is likely outside, per member state. | medium-high | No EU customers; licence only after member-state check, or an EU partner firm. |
| **L5** US: CTA / adviser? | **CTA** under the CEA for a hosted service (commodity options); exemptions narrow; Advisers Act unlikely (commodities). Deribit excludes US persons (verify). | medium | **Exclude US persons** everywhere, waiting list included. |
| **L6** Testnet trial before authorisation? | Testnet uses fake money and no instrument is acquired, so no securities activity takes place; the risk is the **marketing** of a future regulated service and the data processing. A **closed trial for named testers under a written agreement** that says "testnet only, no real money, no offer" is low risk in Brazil. | medium | Trial limited to named testers with the testnet agreement (`drafts/acordo-teste-testnet.md`); no public sign-up. |
| **L7** Performance marketing? | **Don't publish** testnet, replay or stress figures as performance: testnet isn't a market result and hypotheticals carry strict rules (EU Delegated Reg. 2017/565 art. 44; NFA/CFTC 4.41; CVM/ANBIMA practice). Mechanics, rules, risks and the worst case may be shown. Live results only after authorisation, net of fees, standard periods, with warnings. | medium-high | No figures in marketing; in-product figures for testers carry the labels in `drafts/avisos.md`. |
| **L8** Fee by capital? | A fee as % of capital or of profits **reinforces the management reading** and is incompatible with a pure licence; a flat subscription per account is the cleanest. | medium | Flat subscription or per-account fee only. |
| **L9** Waiting list / fake door? | Allowed if it **offers nothing**: interest only, no price for an investment service, no returns, "not available — no service is offered" notice, LGPD consent for contact. A price test of a *software* subscription is safer than of a managed service. | medium | Waiting-list notice in `drafts/avisos.md`; no investment-service price. |
| **L10** AI backtest analysis? | Shown to **our staff** managing their own decision = internal tool, not advice. Shown to a **customer or their adviser** about their account = likely personalised recommendation (*consultoria* / investment advice). | medium | Only for our staff; numbers-only comparison for anyone else. |
| **L11** Help assistant? | **Information, not advice**, if it explains how the product works and what the bot did and why, from published content and the user's own journal, and refuses "should I…", forecasts and personal recommendations. | **L12** EU AI Act? | **Transparency duty** (art. 50): users must be told they're talking to an AI; AI-generated content labelled. Not high-risk. Applies from Aug 2026 (verify). | medium | Label every AI output; log them. |
| **L13** Legal bases? | Accounts, keys, journal, audit log → **contract performance**; security logs → **legitimate interest** + legal obligation (Marco Civil logs); analytics → **consent** (or legitimate interest for strictly first-party, no-identifier counts — country-dependent); surveys → consent. | **L14** Retention vs deletion? | Keep what a **legal duty** requires (Marco Civil 6 months access logs; tax and consumer-claim records ~5 years; if regulated, CVM records ≥ 5 years); delete or anonymise the rest on request or at term end. | **L16** Surveys? | Separate, specific consent if linked to the account; anonymous surveys are simpler. | high | Anonymous by default. |
| **L17** Exchange terms? | Most exchanges allow API trading by the account holder and **restrict sharing credentials or third-party management**; some have broker/partner programmes. Deribit excludes certain residences (US and others — verify the current list). We must read each exchange's current terms. | low (verify) | Only customers the exchange accepts; customer confirms the exchange's terms allow it. |
| **L18** Exchange bans automation? | The customer's relationship with the exchange prevails; our terms must say so, oblige the customer to keep their account in good standing, and describe what happens to open positions (the customer can close them on the exchange at any time; the software stops). | medium | Clause in `drafts/termos-de-uso.md` §8. |
| **L19** Suitability / appropriateness? | Required for regulated parties (Res. CVM 30/2021; MiFID art. 25). Even under a licence, a **knowledge and loss-capacity questionnaire, the risk disclosure and the worst case before activation** are strong defences under the CDC's information duty. Defined-risk structures before retail is a sound policy. | medium-high | Questionnaire + `drafts/termo-ciencia-riscos.md` before connecting; experienced investors only. |
| **L20** Losses and liability? | Market losses can be allocated to the customer (inherent risk, fully disclosed); our own defects can't be fully disclaimed against consumers (CDC art. 25, 51, I). Define the service precisely, exclude the exchange's and third parties' failures, cap liability for B2B/professional users. | medium | `drafts/termos-de-uso.md` §9–§10. |
| **L21** Trade-only key = custody? | **Not custody** (no withdrawal right), but **control over trading** — relevant to the management reading (L1), not to custody/VASP. | medium | Licence model: the customer keeps the key. |
| **L22** Breach duties? | LGPD art. 48 + Res. CD/ANPD 15/2024: notify ANPD and data subjects of incidents with relevant risk within **3 working days** (verify); GDPR art. 33–34: **72 hours**. Keys are high-risk data: encrypted at rest, least privilege, rotation, an incident plan. | medium-high | Incident plan in `data-protection.md` §7. |
| **L23** What can be built before authorisation? | **Everything can be built and used internally** (our own accounts, testnet). External use needs: L6 for testers; L1/L4/L5 for anyone's real money. | medium | Internal only. |
| **L24** Managers as customers' advisers? | An adviser deciding for their client needs **their own registration** (CVM 19 or 21); we must not enable unregistered ones. One change applied to many accounts strengthens the management reading for whoever makes it. | medium | Managers are our staff; per-account changes with the customer's confirmation (matches PM recommendation on #185). |
