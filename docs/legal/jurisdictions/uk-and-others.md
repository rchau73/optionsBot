# United Kingdom and other markets — regulatory notes

> **Not legal advice.** Preparatory notes by the legal-counsel skill. Confidence: **high / medium / low**; **verify** = may have changed after 2023.

## United Kingdom

- **Retail ban on crypto derivatives:** since January 2021 the FCA prohibits the sale, marketing and distribution of derivatives (and, until 2025, ETNs) referencing unregulated crypto-assets to retail consumers (PS20/10). The ban on derivatives remains as far as we know. → **No UK retail customers**, for anything that leads them to crypto options. *(high on the 2021 ban; verify its current scope)*
- **Financial promotions regime for crypto-assets** (since 8 Oct 2023): promotions of qualifying crypto-assets to UK consumers must be made or approved by an authorised person or a registered crypto firm, with risk warnings, a 24-hour cooling-off for first-time investors and appropriateness checks. Our landing page must exclude UK users or carry no promotion. *(medium-high; verify)*
- **Regulated activities:** managing investments and advising on investments (RAO arts. 37, 53) for options = FCA authorisation. *(high)*
- **UK GDPR / DPA 2018:** like GDPR; ICO registration fee for controllers. *(high)*
